# Changes

## 0.3.0 (16 November 2026)

New
- **Counter (POS)** page: scan or search items, quantity +/- and typed boxes, function keys (F2 to F12), several bills
  at once, cash/UPI/card with change to return, save and print the thermal bill with Ctrl+P.
- **Wholesale bill** switch (counter and sale invoice) and a bulk-order suggestion when a bill is big across items,
  such as textile sizes. Wholesale also still applies per item from its minimum quantity.
- **Share panel** on every bill: WhatsApp, Gmail, mail app, text message, download PDF, print as thermal bill or
  A4/A5 invoice.
- **Grow** page: posters (English, Hindi, Tamil), product catalogue PDF, Google profile helper.
- **Second backup place** (pen drive or a cloud-synced folder), backups refreshed every 30 minutes and on closing, and a
  "Check data for damage" button.

Safer
- Damaged data files are refused with a message naming the newest backup; backups are verified before they are kept; a
  damaged backup can never be restored over good data; writes are durable on power loss.
- Pictures in printed pages must be genuine PNG, JPEG or WebP.

Fixed
- Dashboard cards clipped on the left; wide tables pushing pages sideways; POS shortcut row overflow; quantity box kept
  showing ignored letters.

## 0.2.0 (15 November 2026)

Label designs, year-letter barcodes and sizes; owner and staff logins; Windows test installer.
