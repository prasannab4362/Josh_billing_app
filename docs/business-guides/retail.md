# Retail shop / general store

For counter shops selling packed goods to walk-in customers: general stores, supermarkets, gift shops,
footwear and garment shops.

## What matters in this trade

- Fast billing at the counter: **scan a barcode**, take cash or UPI, print a small receipt.
- Prices printed as **MRP, including GST**: the customer pays exactly the price on the pack.
- Most customers are **walk-in**: no name needed.
- Barcode **labels** for items that come without one.
- Low-stock alerts so popular items don't run out.

## Set up

1. Settings › Preferences: GST registered (if you are), **prices include tax**, paper **Thermal 80 mm**
   (or 58 mm), round off on.
2. Items: enter the sale price as the MRP and tick "price includes tax". Type the pack's barcode into
   **Barcode**. For items without one, press **Create** next to Barcode in the item form (or use Print labels: the
   app creates in-store barcodes starting with 2).
3. Items: set a **low-stock alert** on fast movers; they show on the Dashboard.
4. Settings › Print: choose the thermal layout and add a footer such as "Thank you, visit again".

## Everyday work

| Task | Where |
|---|---|
| Counter sale | Sale › New invoice (Alt+S), leave the customer as Cash sale, scan items into the scan box |
| Same item scanned twice | Its quantity goes up by one |
| Paid by UPI / card | Choose the mode under "Received"; a cash sale is always paid in full |
| Print the receipt | Save & print |
| Print price labels | Items › Print labels (label roll or A4 sheet) |
| Goods return | Open the invoice › Sale return (needs a named customer) |
| End-of-day | Reports › Day book; Cash & bank for the drawer balance |

## What was checked in the app (6 of 6 passed)

Example: 3 soaps at ₹45 MRP (18% GST) and 2 packets of tea at ₹140 MRP (5% GST), sold to a walk-in customer by UPI.

- The bill came to **exactly ₹415**, the MRP total. The app worked out the tax inside the price
  (CGST ₹16.97 + SGST ₹16.95).
- The bill was marked **Paid**.
- An item without a barcode got an in-store EAN-13 barcode (`2000000000015`).
- The receipt PDF was made.

## Tips

- A USB or Bluetooth barcode scanner works like a keyboard: click the scan box and scan.
- If a customer wants a GST bill in their name, pick or add them as a party instead of Cash sale.
- Use **Grow** for festival and offer posters to share on WhatsApp.
