# Electrical shop

For electrical and electronics dealers: wires and switches, fans and lights, appliances, motors and pumps,
and supply to electrical contractors.

## What matters in this trade

- **Model and serial numbers** on each appliance sold, for warranty claims.
- **Warranty period** printed on the bill.
- **Wire sold by the coil or by the metre** from one stock.
- **Contractors** on credit, with their GSTIN.

## Set up

1. Settings › Preferences: GST registered, prices **without tax** (or with, if you sell at MRP), paper **A4**.
2. Settings › Extra fields: turn on **Model no.** and **Serial no.**, and turn on **Size** renamed to
   **Warranty**.
3. Items: wire with base unit `m` and second unit `roll` = 90 m (or your coil length). Fans and appliances in `nos`.
4. Tax: most electrical goods are at **18%** (confirm per item with your CA).

## Everyday work

| Task | Where |
|---|---|
| Sell 2 coils | Pick the wire, unit `roll`, qty 2 |
| Sell cut length | Same wire, unit `m`, qty 25 |
| Appliance with serial | Fill Model no., Serial no. (several separated by commas) and Warranty under the line |
| Contractor credit | Pick the contractor; credit limit warning on the invoice |
| Find a sale by serial | Parties › the customer's statement; the bill shows the serials |

## What was checked in the app (5 of 5 passed)

Example: a Gujarat electrical shop selling to a contractor.

- 2 coils of wire at ₹1,700, 25 m at ₹21/m and 2 ceiling fans at ₹2,100 came to **₹9,588.00**
  (CGST ₹731.25 + SGST ₹731.25 at 18%).
- Wire stock fell by **205 m** (2 coils × 90 m + 25 m).
- The model, both serial numbers and "2 years" warranty stayed on the fan line and printed under it.
- The invoice PDF was made.

## Tips

- Turn on **Profit while making a sale** (Settings › Transactions) to see your margin before giving a discount.
- Print barcode labels with the model number for fast billing of switches and fittings.
