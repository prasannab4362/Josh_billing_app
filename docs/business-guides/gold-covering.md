# Gold covering / imitation jewellery

For shops selling gold-plated (covering), one-gram gold, fashion and imitation jewellery, at retail and to
resellers.

## What matters in this trade

- **Many designs**, each with a design number, sold **by the piece, pair or set**.
- **MRP** prices with GST inside at the counter.
- A **lower price for resellers** buying a dozen or more.
- Fast billing by **barcode labels** stuck on each tray or packet.
- **Posters** of new designs to share on WhatsApp.

## Set up

1. Settings › Preferences: GST registered, prices **include tax**, paper **Thermal 80 mm** (A4 for reseller bills).
2. Items: one item per design (`Covering Bangles (pair) D-101`), unit `pcs` or `set`, HSN **7117**, GST **3%**.
3. Items: **Wholesale price** and minimum qty (for example 12 pairs) for resellers.
4. Print barcode labels with the design number and price; stick them on each packet.
5. Settings › Tax rates: add **1.5%** if you sell handcrafted imitation jewellery that qualifies for it.

## GST note

Imitation jewellery (HSN 7117) is commonly taxed at **3%**. Some sources mention 1.5% for handcrafted pieces
from September 2025, and older sources still quote other rates. Confirm with your CA.

## Everyday work

| Task | Where |
|---|---|
| Counter sale | Scan the packets; walk-in; UPI |
| Reseller order | Pick the reseller; the wholesale price applies from 12 pieces |
| New design photos | Items › photo; Grow › Catalogue makes a PDF catalogue to share |
| Festival offers | Grow › Posters (English, Hindi, Tamil) |
| Slow designs | Reports › Item-wise sales and profit |

## What was checked in the app (6 of 6 passed)

Example: a Telangana gold covering shop.

- Counter sale of a pair of bangles (₹450 MRP each pair, 2 pairs) and a temple necklace set (₹1,850): total
  **₹2,750.00**, exactly the MRP total (CGST ₹40.05 + SGST ₹40.04 at 3%).
- 24 pairs to a reseller at the wholesale price of ₹320: total **₹7,680.00** (CGST ₹111.85 + SGST ₹111.84).
- Stock of the design fell by **26 pairs**.
- The receipt PDF was made.

## Tips

- Keep the design number in the item name or **Item code** so it prints on labels and bills.
- Use Items › Import from Excel when a new collection arrives.
