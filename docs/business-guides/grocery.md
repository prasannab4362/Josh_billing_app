# Grocery / kirana store

For neighbourhood grocery shops selling loose grains, pulses, oil, spices and packed goods, often on
monthly credit (khata).

## What matters in this trade

- Selling **loose items by weight**: 750 g, 1.25 kg, 5.5 kg.
- A mix of **GST-free** loose staples and packed goods with GST.
- **Khata**: regular families buy on credit and pay weekly or monthly, not bill by bill.
- Small thermal receipts and quick billing.

## Set up

1. Settings › Preferences: prices **include tax**, paper **Thermal 58 mm** or 80 mm, round off on.
   If your turnover is below the GST limit and you are not registered, switch GST registration off: no GST
   is charged then.
2. Items: loose items in `kg` with the price per kg. Packed items in `pkt` / `pcs` / `btl`.
3. Tax: loose cereals and pulses are usually exempt (use GST 0% or Exempted). Packed and labelled food and
   edible oil commonly 5%. Confirm with your CA.
4. Parties: add each khata family with a phone number. A credit limit is optional.

## Everyday work

| Task | Where |
|---|---|
| Weigh and sell | Type the weight as a decimal: 0.75 for 750 g, 5.5 for 5½ kg |
| Khata purchase | Pick the family as the customer and save without "Received" |
| Khata payment | Payments › Payment in. Leave it unallocated, or set it against bills; the balance updates either way |
| Remind a family | Parties › the family › WhatsApp reminder (the message opens in WhatsApp for you to send) |
| What each family owes | Parties list, or Reports › Receivables ageing |
| Restock | Purchase bills; low-stock alerts on the Dashboard |

## What was checked in the app (5 of 5 passed)

Example: a Tamil Nadu kirana. A khata customer bought 5.5 kg loose rice at ₹58/kg, 750 g moong dal at ₹124/kg
and 2 pouches of groundnut oil at ₹195 (5% GST included).

- Total **₹802.00**, with GST only on the oil (CGST ₹9.29 + SGST ₹9.28).
- Stock fell by the exact weight: 94.5 kg rice and 24.25 kg dal left.
- A ₹300 khata payment not set against any bill reduced the family's balance correctly.
- The receipt PDF was made.

## Tips

- Quantities keep three decimals, so 0.125 kg (125 g) is fine.
- For staples you buy in 25/50 kg bags and sell loose, keep stock in kg and buy in a second unit `bag`.
- Use Reports › Item-wise sales and profit to see which items earn the most.
